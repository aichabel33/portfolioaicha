Lysi · Orderful · NetSuite

Order-to-Cash EDI Between NetSuite and Trading Partners

Connecting NetSuite to trading partners across North America, Europe and Oceania so orders, fulfillments and invoices flow without manual entry.

Challenge

Each partner expects its own X12 or EDIFACT variant, business rules and units of measure, while NetSuite needs clean sales orders, item fulfillments and invoices.

Solution

JSONata scripts encode client rules, Orderful Mosaic handles mapping and NetSuite loading with UoM conversion, the rule engine captures partner requirements, and custom JavaScript drives NetSuite workflows — all tested end to end before go-live.
